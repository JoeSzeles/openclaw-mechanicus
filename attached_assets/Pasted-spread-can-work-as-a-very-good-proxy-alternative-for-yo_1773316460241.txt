Why Volume Is Often Missing/Zero on IG CFDs via Lightstreamer
IG's streaming feed (Lightstreamer) for many CFD instruments (particularly spot metals like gold/silver, forex, some indices) does not provide actual traded volume or turnover data in the tick updates.
Common fields in a price subscription (BID, OFFER, MID_PRICE, CHANGE, UPDATE_TIME, MARKET_STATE, etc.) are there, but volume/trade count is frequently absent or always 0:

IG CFDs are OTC (over-the-counter) instruments — they don't trade on a central exchange with transparent volume.
What you see is IG's internal bid/ask quotes + last price.
Real volume would require exchange data (e.g. COMEX for gold futures), but IG doesn't forward that for spot CFDs.
Many users report the same on forums (TradingView, ProRealTime, GitHub issues for trading-ig lib): vol=0 or no volume field at all.

Your log (vol=0) is typical — not a bug, just how IG streams spot CFDs.
How Spread Can Replace Volume in the Antenna
Spread behaves as a liquidity/volatility proxy and often correlates with volume-like signals your antenna is looking for:

Tight spread (e.g. 0.3–1.5 on gold) → high liquidity, normal market, lots of participants quoting → similar to "high volume" feel.
Wide/spiking spread (e.g. 4+ → 10+) → low liquidity, uncertainty, news/volatility spikes, or thin order book → mimics "volume surge" or "absorption" (big players defending levels while quotes widen).
Spread contraction after expansion → liquidity returning, potential reversal/breakout.
Persistent wide spread → potential flash-crash risk or illiquid trap.

These are exactly the kinds of patterns your Volume Antenna targets (flash crashes, absorption, divergences). So spread dynamics can detect similar "market stress" or "order flow imbalance" without real vol.
Suggested Implementation in BrainJar / Volume Antenna
Replace or supplement vol-based logic with spread-based features:

Track spread delta / rate of change
Compute: current_spread - previous_spread
Spike > threshold (e.g. +2 points in 1–5 seconds) → flag as "absorption" or "vol surge proxy" → excite certain antenna neurons.

Spread threshold filter
Ignore signals if spread > max_acceptable (e.g. 6–8 points) → avoids trading in illiquid/noise.
Or: high spread + price stagnation → "pain" stimulus (market fighting back).

Spread divergence
Spread widening while price flat → potential reversal (order book thinning).
Spread tightening on momentum → confirmation (liquidity supporting move).

Poisson encoding for spread
Feed spread_delta or normalized_spread as additional input stream to sensory neurons (parallel to price_delta).
Example: higher spread → higher spike rate in "stress" subpopulation → Cortex interprets as caution/rejection.
